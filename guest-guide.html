A COMFORTABLE START TO THE DAY

Hot Water

The three kitchen controls

Hot water is automatically heated overnight using night-rate electricity. Normally you do not need to change anything. The controls are on the kitchen wall. The main rocker switches should normally remain ON.

1

2 Hour Boost

LEFT — CHOSRY. Press the small power-symbol button for extra hot water during the day. This does not change the normal overnight programme. Leave the main rocker switch ON.

2

Bath / Sink

CENTRE. The left selector is Bath / Sink; the right is Off / On and should normally stay ON.
Sink heats approximately half the cylinder: quicker, for a smaller amount such as a quick shower.
Bath heats the full cylinder, useful for several showers. There is no bath here — the labels describe how much water is heated.

3

1 Hour Boost

RIGHT — CHOSRY. Press the small power-symbol button to boost the en-suite towel radiator. Leave the main rocker switch ON.

A tip for consecutive showers

There is normally no need to turn the shower flow to maximum. A comfortable moderate flow makes stored hot water last considerably longer, especially for consecutive showers. Shower time varies with flow, temperature and the hot water available.

FEEL AT HOME

Heating

Room heating is automatic and may normally be off in warmer summer weather. Typical targets are approximately 22°C in the living/lounge area and 18°C in bedrooms and the hallway.

Use the digital radiator thermostat’s + / − controls for a local adjustment. The system may later return to its programmed levels.

Close windows before heating

Heating may switch off automatically in an area with an open window. If the apartment feels unusually cold, contact us or the local team rather than changing electrical timers.

FRESH AIR, WITH CARE

Windows & Child Safety

Our heritage-style windows retain the building’s character with modern double glazing. The rear windows have child-safety restrictors limiting normal opening to approximately 100 mm.

Adults can release the restrictors using the brass window toggles when a wider opening is needed. Please restore them afterwards, particularly when children are staying.

Opening front and rear windows briefly creates an effective through-draught. Please do not leave windows fully open while the apartment is unattended.

Dunmore East has a healthy population of seabirds — we would prefer that they admire Harbour House from outside!

MEET THE JAMES BOND FAN

Shower Ventilation

Switch on the main en-suite light switch outside the door and the powered ventilation shutter gradually opens. The circular aperture looks a little like a James Bond title sequence — hence our “James Bond fan”!

This is normal. The shutter helps reduce backdraughts and cold wind, preserving warmth when the fan is not operating.

Use the extractor whenever showering and allow it to clear moisture afterwards. You can also open a window when the weather permits.

A LITTLE EVERYDAY COMFORT

Towel Radiator

The heated towel radiator is in the en-suite off the master bedroom. It comes on automatically for approximately one hour from around 5:00 am.

For warmth at another time, press the small power-symbol button on the right-hand CHOSRY “1 Hour Boost” unit on the kitchen wall. Its main rocker switch should remain ON.

STAY CONNECTED

Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi is provided. The current connection details are displayed inside the apartment.

Please leave the equipment powered and connected, including the power connection behind the sofa. Avoid unplugging it, removing cables, resetting it or changing its configuration.

If you cannot connect, contact the owner or Dunmore East Escapes. We will help you get online.

MAKE SOMETHING LOVELY

Cooking & Extractor

When cooking, pull or slide the cooker hood out over the cooker and use its fan control. The extractor removes steam, moisture and odours and helps protect the apartment from humidity.

The useful lights underneath the kitchen cupboards have switches on the wall. Please leave supplies intended to remain available switched on.

PRIVACY & DAYLIGHT

Blinds

Please use the side chains or cord controls rather than pulling directly on the blind fabric, including the Roman blinds. This protects their mechanisms and mountings.

En-suite day-and-night blind

Only a small movement of the side chain is needed. Moving the striped sections changes privacy and incoming daylight, so you can keep privacy while still letting daylight in. Please do not tug the fabric.

A SMALL HELP AT THE END OF YOUR STAY

Waste & Glass Recycling

Bins are at the rear of the apartment; the waste provider is Oxygen. The green bin takes suitable recyclables such as clean paper/cardboard and other material accepted in that stream. Use the black/general refuse bin for ordinary household refuse.

Glass bottles go to a bottle bank

Please do not put glass bottles in the normal rear bins. Use an appropriate local bottle bank before departure where reasonably possible. Ask Dunmore East Escapes for directions.

No transport, or unable to take them?

Leave bottles neatly together and let Dunmore East Escapes know so they can arrange to deal with them. Keep glass out of the normal bins.

FOR LITTLE TIDY-UPS

Dyson Vacuum

A cordless Dyson vacuum is provided in the hallway.

After use, return it to its charging cradle, make sure it is correctly seated and leave the charging supply ON. It will then be ready for the next guest.

ARRIVING & GETTING AROUND

Parking/CCTV

Parking is at the vehicle owner’s risk. Security CCTV operates in common areas of the development, but does not guarantee vehicle security.

There has not historically been a particular security problem. Normal precautions are still sensible: please avoid leaving valuables visibly displayed inside vehicles.

THANK YOU FOR LOOKING AFTER THE APARTMENT.

House Rules

Relax and enjoy Harbour House, remembering that it is part of a residential development.

  • No parties; please respect our neighbours and keep noise considerate, especially in the evenings and at night.
  • No additional visitors or overnight guests without prior host agreement.
  • No smoking or vaping indoors, including through open windows. Please go outside the building to an appropriate external location. Smoke or vapour can affect neighbours and activate detection systems.
  • Leave Wi-Fi and fixed equipment connected; please do not reset or reconfigure them.
  • Restore child-safety restrictors and do not leave windows fully open when out.
  • Use bottle banks for glass and chains/cords for blinds.
  • If something is not working, please tell us.

WE ARE HERE TO HELP

Equipment & Electrical Help

If something unusual happens — including an electrical protection device tripping — please let us or Dunmore East Escapes know.

Please do not dismantle, reset or reconfigure electrical equipment, Wi-Fi, heating controls, timers or other fixed equipment. The radiator’s normal + / − temperature adjustment is fine. We can arrange the right help.
